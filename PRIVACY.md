# Jabbersnap for Windows privacy policy

The full Windows app and the Mac app share the
[Jabbersnap privacy policy](https://github.com/20twenty/jabbersnap-mac/blob/main/PRIVACY.md),
covering local History, cloud AI requests, key storage and optional usage
statistics. It applies to Jabbersnap for Windows 0.8.16 and later.

For Windows Lite 0.1.x, see the
[Lite privacy policy](PRIVACY-LITE.md). Lite is retired; its policy does not
describe the full app.
